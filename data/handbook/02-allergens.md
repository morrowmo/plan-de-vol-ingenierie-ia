# Allergens and special diets

All our cakes are baked in a kitchen that also uses nuts, so we cannot guarantee that any item is nut-free.
The Gluten-Free Brownie is made with almond flour and contains no wheat, but it does contain nuts and eggs.
The Vegan Banana Bread contains no eggs, milk or butter. It contains gluten.
We offer oat milk and soy milk for any coffee, at no extra cost.
The Tomato Soup is vegan and gluten-free when served without bread.
If a customer has a serious allergy, staff must ask the manager before serving.
