# Opening hours

Café Comet is open Monday to Friday from 7:30 to 18:00.
On Saturday we open from 8:30 to 19:00.
On Sunday we open from 9:00 to 14:00, for brunch only.
We are closed on public holidays, except for the 14th of July, when we open from 10:00 to 16:00.
The kitchen stops serving hot food 30 minutes before closing time.
