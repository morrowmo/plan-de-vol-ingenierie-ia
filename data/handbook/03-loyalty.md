# Loyalty card

Every customer can ask for a free paper loyalty card at the counter.
Each hot drink earns one stamp. After nine stamps, the tenth hot drink is free.
Stamps cannot be exchanged for cash and the free drink cannot be a cake or a meal.
Lost loyalty cards cannot be replaced, so we suggest customers take a photo of their card.
On their birthday, loyalty card holders get a free croissant, if they show an ID.
