# Staff rules

Staff must wash their hands before every shift and after every break.
Each staff member gets one free drink and one free pastry per shift.
Breaks last 15 minutes for a shift of four hours, and 30 minutes for a longer shift.
Phones stay in the locker during service, except for the manager.
If a customer complains, staff stay calm, apologise, and offer to remake the order for free.
The café dog, Pixel, is not allowed behind the counter.
