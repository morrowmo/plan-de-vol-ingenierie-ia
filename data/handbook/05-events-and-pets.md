# Events, groups and pets

Dogs are welcome on the terrace and inside the café, on a lead. We give them a free bowl of water.
Groups of more than eight people must book a table at least two days in advance.
The café can be rented for private events on Sunday afternoons, from 15:00 to 20:00. The price is 250 euros, including coffee and tea for up to 30 guests.
Every first Thursday of the month, we host a board-game evening from 18:30 to 21:30. Entry is free.
Live music is not allowed, because our neighbours asked us to keep the noise down.
